## Protection

### Glasses over glasses
I have order the the following glasses to wear over my glasses:
- (Ochranné brýle přes brýle UVEX Super OTG, čiré)[https://www.vochoc.cz/ochranne-bryle/ochranne-bryle-pres-bryle-uvex-super-otg-cire_OC_9169065.php]
- (Ochranné brýle přes brýle UVEX Super OTG, tmavé)[https://www.vochoc.cz/ochranne-bryle/ochranne-bryle-pres-bryle-uvex-super-otg-tmave_OC_9169081.php]

They provide protection acording to (EN 166 F)[https://www.wiseworksafe.com/blog/view/understanding-en-166-personal-eye-protection-standard]. The F stands for low-energy impact, (6mm steel ball at 45 m/s). However, glasses sold as "ballistic" in army shops often seem to have the same protection.

These glasses, (uvex-ultrasonic-planet)[https://www.uvex-safety.cz/cs/produkty/ochranne-bryle/9505/uzavrene-bryle-uvex-ultrasonic-planet/], provide higher protection and also look cool, might be worth a try in the future.
